## The science behind Quad 4

Eyebrow: THE SCIENCE BEHIND QUAD 4

### Four Ingredients. Different Mechanisms.

**01 — POWER · Sildenafil**
The Established Foundation.
One of the most extensively studied medications for erectile dysfunction, with more than 25 years of clinical use.
Sildenafil is a PDE5 inhibitor that helps improve erectile function by supporting blood flow during sexual stimulation.

**02 — SPEED · Vardenafil**
A Different PDE5 Option.
Vardenafil is another established PDE5 inhibitor used to treat erectile dysfunction.
Although it has a different pharmacological profile from sildenafil, faster onset or greater effectiveness when combined in Quad 4 has not been demonstrated.

**03 — LONGER-ACTING · Tadalafil**
The Extended Window.
Tadalafil is known for its longer duration of action.
Certain FDA-approved tadalafil formulations can remain effective for up to 36 hours, offering greater flexibility in the timing of sexual activity.
This duration has not been established for the combined Quad 4 formulation.

**04 — THE BRAIN · Apomorphine**
A New Dimension in Men's Sexual Health.
Apomorphine works through dopamine receptors in the brain, targeting central nervous system pathways involved in sexual arousal and response.

Four Ingredients. Distinct Properties. One Innovative Approach.

## FAQ

Eyebrow: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Everything You Want to Know.

**What is Quad 4?**
Quad 4 is a proposed compounded formulation containing sildenafil, vardenafil, tadalafil, and apomorphine.

**Can I take Quad 4 with other ED medications?**
Combining PDE5 inhibitors can increase adverse-effect risks. Quad 4 already contains three PDE5 inhibitors, so its proposed formulation requires particular medical scrutiny. Do not combine ED medications without explicit guidance from your prescribing clinician.

**Can I get a prescription online?**
A licensed healthcare provider may prescribe an appropriate medication after evaluating your medical history and clinical needs, subject to applicable regulations.

**Will my medication arrive discreetly?**
Discreet home delivery may be available for prescribed treatments through participating licensed pharmacies.
