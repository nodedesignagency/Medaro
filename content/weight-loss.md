## GLP-1 education

Eyebrow: GLP-1 EDUCATION

### UNDERSTAND YOUR OPTIONS

**What are GLP-1 medications?**
GLP-1 medications work on pathways involved in appetite and blood-sugar regulation. Certain FDA-approved medications are used for chronic weight management in eligible patients.

**How do I know if I'm eligible?**
Eligibility depends on factors such as your weight, medical history, existing health conditions, current medications, and a provider's clinical assessment.

**Is medication enough on its own?**
Prescription weight-loss treatment is generally intended to be combined with changes in nutrition, physical activity, and other healthy habits.

**Are there risks?**
Yes. GLP-1 medications can cause side effects and carry important warnings. A healthcare provider should review potential benefits, risks, contraindications, and alternatives before prescribing.

## FAQ

Eyebrow: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS · YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

### Get the Clarity You Deserve.

**Do I need an in-person appointment?**
Many steps can take place online. Your provider will determine whether a virtual evaluation is appropriate or whether additional testing or an in-person assessment is necessary.

**Is a prescription guaranteed?**
No. Prescriptions are issued only when a licensed healthcare provider determines that treatment is medically appropriate.

**Does everyone qualify for GLP-1 treatment?**
No. Eligibility depends on individual medical factors, prescribing criteria, and treatment availability.

**Will I lose weight quickly?**
Weight-loss results vary by person, medication, adherence, and other health factors. No specific amount or rate of weight loss can be guaranteed.

**Can medication be delivered to my home?**
When available and prescribed, medications may be fulfilled through a licensed pharmacy offering home delivery.

**What if GLP-1 treatment isn't right for me?**
Your provider may discuss alternative weight-management approaches depending on your individual health needs.

**Are GLP-1 medications safe?**
FDA-approved GLP-1 medications have established prescribing information describing their benefits, risks, and approved uses. They can cause side effects and are not appropriate for everyone. A licensed healthcare provider should evaluate your medical history before treatment.
