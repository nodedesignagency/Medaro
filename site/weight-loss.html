Explore Your Treatment Options

  • What are GLP-1 medications?

    GLP-1 medications work on pathways involved in appetite and blood-sugar regulation. Certain FDA-approved medications are used for chronic weight management in eligible patients.

  • How do I know if I'm eligible?

    Eligibility depends on factors such as your weight, medical history, existing health conditions, current medications, and a provider's clinical assessment.

  • Is medication enough on its own?

    Prescription weight-loss treatment is generally intended to be combined with changes in nutrition, physical activity, and other healthy habits.

  • Are there risks?

    Yes. GLP-1 medications can cause side effects and carry important warnings. A healthcare provider should review potential benefits, risks, contraindications, and alternatives before prescribing.
